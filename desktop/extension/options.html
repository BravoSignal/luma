luma

YOUR MUSIC. ONE MOVE AWAY.

Your music.
On your desktop.

This extension connects YouTube Music to Luma Desktop. Install the desktop app, open it, then pair them once.

  1. 1

    Install Luma Desktop

    The desktop app displays your widget. Choose your computer below.

    Mac · Apple SiliconM-series chipMac · IntelIntel processorWindows · x64Intel / AMD

    On a Mac, check Apple menu → About This Mac to find your chip.

    Installation help

    Mac: unzip, move Luma.app to Applications and open it. Luma lives in the top menu bar, with no Dock icon. The current preview is unsigned and not notarized.

    Windows: extract the entire ZIP to a permanent folder, then run Luma.exe. Keep its other files together. Find Luma in the system tray.

    All desktop downloads ↗
  2. 2

    Open Luma and copy your code

    Open Connection in the desktop app and choose Copy pairing code. Keep Luma running.

  3. 3

    Pair with your browser

    Open YouTube Music ↗

    Once connected, reload your existing YouTube Music tab and play a track. Use Luma to choose a screen zone or hold shortcut.

The connection stays on your computer. No Luma account or extra Google sign-in. Pair each computer separately.
