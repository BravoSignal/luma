    Once connected, reload your existing YouTube Music tab and play a track. Use Luma to choose a screen zone or hold shortcut.

Reload YouTube Music after installing or updating Bridge

Already have YouTube Music open? Refresh that tab once so the new music controls become active. Pairing alone is not enough. New Bridge versions also try to refresh open music tabs automatically.

Pause music for videos

Allow detection on websites once, then exclude any sites where music should keep playing. YouTube Music is always excluded. Nothing is recorded.

What does website access do?

Chrome asks for access to HTTP and HTTPS websites. Luma checks only whether audio or video is playing and sends anonymous activity flags to the desktop app on this computer. It does not record media or send page text, URLs or titles. Previously allowed sites keep working until you enable all-site access.

Excluded websites

    An exclusion includes its subdomains and embedded players on that website. Reload an already open video tab after updating the extension. Muted-media and visible-tab rules are configured in Luma.

    The connection stays on your computer. No Luma account or extra Google sign-in. Pair each computer separately.
